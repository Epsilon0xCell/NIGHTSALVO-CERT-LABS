# Tier ladder

Tiers are counted only on completed labs: a lab counts when its
solution was executed and verified in session and its full writeup is
published in this repo. Tier moves are announced with the lab that
caused them.

| Tier | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| SCOUT | Repo live, 0 labs. The starting tier. |
| INITIATE | 5 labs across at least 2 cert tracks |
| OPERATOR | 15 labs across at least 3 cert tracks |
| BREAKER | 30 labs across at least 4 cert tracks, at least 3 capstone labs |
| SALVO | 50 labs across at least 5 tracks, at least 6 capstones |
| ELITE | 75 labs across at least 6 tracks, at least 10 capstones, at least 1 multi-lab scenario arc per track |
| MYTH | 120 labs |
| LEGEND | 200 labs |
| CANON | Conferred, never counted. |

A capstone lab is a multi-stage scenario that chains at least three
skills from its track into one investigation. A scenario arc is a set
of linked capstones sharing one environment and one storyline.
